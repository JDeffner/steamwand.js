How to use this

A hand-test surface over the whole binding. It drives your real Steam client, so writes are real writes.

  1. Start the Steam client and log in.
  2. Press init. Nothing reaches Steam before that.
  3. Use Workshop for the wrapped flows, or Raw call for any of the 801 generated methods.
  4. Watch the log at the bottom right. Every call, callback, and error lands there.

App id. 480 is Spacewar, Valve's public test app, and works on any account. Use your own app id to reach your own workshop items.

One init per process. Steam cannot start again after close. To begin a new session, stop the script and run pnpm workbench again.
